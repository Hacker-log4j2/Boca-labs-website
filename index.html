What it is

One device.
Two jobs.

Boca Block is being developed to make needle positioning for the IAN block simpler and more consistent. It replaces part of the freehand estimate with a physical reference you can feel and see.

01

Holds the bite

Upper and lower bite surfaces seat on the posterior teeth, like a conventional bite block. Ridged traction keeps them from sliding.

02

Guides the needle

An integrated guide presents the needle at the intended approach angle once the block is seated, so the starting trajectory is repeatable.

How it works

Seat

You stay in control. Landmarks, patient suitability, needle depth, aspiration and technique remain the clinician's call. Illustration is simplified.

Adjustable

Turns to fit
the patient.

The guide pivots on its point of rotation from −8° to +8°, and a handle lets you set the angle. That range lets one design adjust to different patients and arch shapes.

Material

Clear. Flexible.
ISO-compliant.

Boca Block is made in BioMed flexible resin, an ISO-compliant material. It's clear, so you can see the needle and treatment area through it, with enough give to seat comfortably on the teeth.

Design details

Every part has a job.

Slotted viewing windows

Openings along the guide let you see the needle pass through, estimate how far it has advanced, and lift the device away without threading the needle back through a closed tube.

Ridged bite surfaces

Traction ridges reduce sliding between the teeth and the block.

Alignment notch

A small triangular notch gives a visual and tactile reference for seating it against the teeth.

Handle

Gives you a grip to control the angle while you position the guide.

Point of rotation

The guide pivots −8° to +8° so the angle can be adjusted per patient.

Left and right versions

Current prototypes come as separate left-side and right-side devices marked L and R. You pick the one for the side you're injecting. A single universal design may replace them later. Subject to change.
